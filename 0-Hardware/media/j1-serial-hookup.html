J1 Serial Console

Solder a 2.54 mm male pin header onto the Lidl Silvercrest and connect a 3.3 V USB-to-serial adapter with Dupont wires. Three wires, no power from the adapter.

    Simplified diagram, not to scale, for the Lidl Silvercrest. Pin 1 is at the bottom. Adapter pin order varies between models: follow its silkscreen, not the order shown here. The Sengled G4 has a different connector, see 0-Hardware/sengled-e39-g8c/.
